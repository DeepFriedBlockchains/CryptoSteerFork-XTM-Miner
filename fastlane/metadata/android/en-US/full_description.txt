CryptoSteer XMR Miner runs a native RandomX mining engine against pools selected by the user. Mining is always user initiated and remains visible through an Android foreground-service notification.

Features include pool profiles and failover, TLS pool connections, live hashrate history, thermal and battery safeguards, tuning profiles, benchmark tools, encrypted pool credentials, and optional account-based remote rig control.

The disclosed developer contribution starts at 2% of active mining time and becomes 1% after 30 cumulative days for signed-in accounts. The F-Droid build does not contain the private APK updater and does not send anonymous installation or mining telemetry.

Mining places sustained load on the processor and can increase heat, power use, and battery wear. Use only devices you own or are authorized to operate.
