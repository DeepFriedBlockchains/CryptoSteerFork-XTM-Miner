### Summary

Native, user-controlled RandomX Monero miner for Android.

### Description

CryptoSteer XMR Miner is a native Android RandomX miner published by THE CRYPTO STEER LLC. Mining is started by the user and remains visible through a foreground-service notification. The application supports user-selected pools, TLS, pool profiles, live statistics, device safeguards, benchmarking, tuning, and optional account-based remote-rig controls.

The F-Droid build removes the APK installation permission and update receiver, disables the private updater, and disables anonymous installation and mining telemetry at compile time. Native dependencies are pinned and published as source. The application discloses a developer contribution of 2% of active mining time, reduced to 1% after the documented threshold.
